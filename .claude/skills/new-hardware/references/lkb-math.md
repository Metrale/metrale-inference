# The LKB, in math

book is the single copy; this stub keeps the skill's links (e.g. "§2", "§3") working: the section
numbers there are the same.

The rules a campaign applies, in one line each:
- §2: bit parity across classes holds exactly when two kernels run the same reduction tree
  (declared in `KERNEL_FAMILIES.toml` `[[reduction]]`; `met circuit lkb` prints the verdict);
- §3: a Tier 2 tolerance is derived (per-op error, measured amplification, composed), never
  guessed;
- §5: time and energy gains of a fusion are measured separately;
- §6: the equality-saturation fuser is an experiment, kept off until it wins on the golden plans.
