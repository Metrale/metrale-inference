# Hardware Beachhead Campaign: H100

- **Campaign PR:** "Hardware Beachhead Campaign: H100" (this file's PR).
- **Branch / base:** `feat/new-hardware-kit` on `feat/ml-utils` (PR #100), which carries
  `met ml-utils` and `met serve --mock`. Both are drafts; neither merges until the H100 work
  proves them.
- **Devices:** `h100-sxm` (first), then `h200-sxm` and `h100-nvl`. **Kernel class:** `kernels/hopper/`.
- **Status:** code-free preparation done (this PR); nothing below has run on an H100 unless a
  line says so.
- **LKB baseline** (`references/lkb.md`): the dense 27B's h100-sxm golden plan puts 100 % of
  the step on registered families at C1/C16/C128 and 0 % inside the evidence envelope (no
  Hopper records); residual 17 kernels / 4062 lines in `kernels/hopper/common/`, none modelled
  by a rule. Every exit report states "Promoted into the LKB" and "Residual delta".
- **Loop budget per model:** 3 calendar days or 40 device-hours of loop measurement, whichever
  comes first, then escalate (`references/improvement-loop.md`, "The loop is bounded"). A
  proposal: whoever owns the campaign may change it here and in the ledger.
- **Done means:** for each model in section 4, bit parity is recorded (Tiers 1-3), the
  improvement loop's exit criterion holds on a
  same-box H100 scoreboard against vLLM (tok/s at every rung C1-C128, J/tok at every rung but at
  most one small mid-ladder loss); the accuracy bar holds; `[benchmarks.limits]` are measured
  and declared; the tensor-core policy's backlog is either closed or measured; the hopper gates
  are certified.

> **BIT PARITY FIRST, THEN PERFORMANCE.** No timing on the H100 counts until bit parity is
> recorded (Tier 1 on the mock; Tiers 1 and 2 on the real model; `references/bit-parity.md`),
> and every improvement-loop iteration keeps it. Inherited gb10 kernels that keep their launch
> shape and reduction order on Hopper must be byte-identical to the GB10 reference box at the
> op level; split-K counts derived from 132 SMs, Hopper's own tiles and cuBLASLt calls get the
> declared tolerance and the transcript match rate instead. Record TTBP and TTPV in
> `ledger/h100.toml`.
>
> **SPEED AND ENERGY ARE SEPARATE METRICS.** HBM3 at 3.35 TB/s and a ~700 W board make the
> trade-off sharper than on GB10: a lever that halves a step's time can still raise J/tok if it
> more than doubles the above-idle power. Report tok/s, TTFT and counter-based J/tok for every
> rung; record TTPV-speed and TTPV-energy separately (`references/speed-and-energy.md`).

## 2. Audit

1. **The golden plans are 100 % roofline projection.** Every h100-sxm plan reports "Shared 0.0 %
   (measured on this class)" at C1, C16 and C128 (e.g. `kernels/circuits/plans/hw/
   qwen3.8-27b-nvfp4--h100-sxm.md` lines 118, 158, 198). For the dense 27B NVFP4 at the
   recipe's tier, every matmul at C1 plans onto `compute=cuda_core` (W4A16 GEMVs, BF16 head
   GEMV). At C16 and C128 the GDN and attention projections plan onto the W4A16 tensor-core
   tiers (`mma.sync.m16n8k16.bf16`), but the dense FFN, the largest op (24.9 % / 9.8 %), has
   **no rule of this class** (on GB10 it runs the FP4 MMQ tiles, absent here), and the GDN
   recurrence and paged attention (73 % of the C128 step) are CUDA-core.
2. **No tensor-core policy was enforced.** Before this PR `kernels/hopper/HARDWARE.toml` had no
   `[tensor_core_policy]` (the plans said "nothing is enforced on this class"), so a regression
   to CUDA cores was invisible to `met circuit plan --matrix ... --check`. This PR adds one
   (section 6); its backlog entries are the class's tensor-core work list.
3. **No `[benchmarks.limits]`.** `met benchmark certify` (`crates/server/src/cli/bench_certify/
   mod.rs:101`) and every self-serving gate run (`plan_serve`, `crates/server/src/cli/
   bench_serve_plan.rs:196`, used by `met benchmark run --pull-request-gate`) refuse the class.
   A plain `met benchmark run` against a running server proceeds, but its thermal pre-check
   records temperatures without judging them (`bench_run.rs` `temp_ceilings`). Measure on the
   H100, then declare; this PR deliberately invents none.
4. **`met circuit show` and `met circuit venn` could not target a device**; only `plan` and
   `display` took `--hardware`. This PR adds it to both (section 6).
5. **W8A8 prefill caps are unmeasured.** `w8a8_prefill_max_m_widening` and `_narrowing` are
   `4294967295` (u32::MAX, "no cap") in `kernels/hopper/HARDWARE.toml` (lines 362-363), the
   baseline value, against GB10's measured 64 / 384. Measure the crossover on the H100.
6. **The planner does not see the class's own kernels.** None of the hopper kernels listed in
   section 1 appears in any `FUSIONS.toml` rule or `KERNEL_FAMILIES.toml` family, so the golden
   plans (and the tensor-core policy and the Venn) describe gb10's routing, not what the runtime
   runs: e.g. the plans put the BF16 head at 5-16 rows on `dense_gemv_bf16` (CUDA core), while
   `lm_head_m16_tc = true` routes it to `dense_gemm_m16_bf16` (tensor core). Modelling them in a
   hopper `FUSIONS.toml` / `KERNEL_FAMILIES.toml` overlay, each rule citing its dispatch site,
   is the first planner task. Its follow-on, found on the first H100 session: `met circuit
   diff` has no hopper instance (the circuit executor embeds gb10's rules and
   `kernels/circuits/INSTANCES.toml` has no hopper target), so the Tier 1 circuit-vs-legacy
   check is N/A on Hopper and the Hopper serve runs `forward=legacy` until it exists.
7. **Declared-precision gaps with no rule.** At `--precision declared`, the h100-sxm plans have
   no rule of the class for: the dense 27B's W4A4 MLP (28.7 % of the C1 step, 21.1 % at C16)
   and its FP8 head; the FP8 35B's routed experts at every row count (9-45 % per projection); the NVFP4 35B's
   FP8-per-tensor GDN and attention projections (`gdn.qkvz` 30.5 % at C1). These are "policy
   variant" or "shared-unmeasured" on the Venn (a family implements the op; no rule routes it).
8. **`sm_count` is a class constant but a device fact.** `h100-nvl` lists 132 SMs with a
   conflicting source at 114 (`kernels/DEVICES.toml`); the value also exists in HARDWARE.toml.

## 3. What transfers from GB10, and what does not

| Work | Transfers? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Inherited gb10 kernels (SM80-class floor: `mma.sync`, `cp.async`, no TMA) | as code, yes | they assemble for `sm_90a` (`docs/HARDWARE.md`: 1282/1282 under `--strict`); none is measured on H100, and an `mma.sync` kernel caps well below what `wgmma` reaches on this class |
| FP4 MMQ tiles, W4A4 MX GEMVs (`mma.sync.m16n8k64.mxf4nvf4.block_scale`) | **no** | Blackwell-only instruction; guarded out; `[expected_absent]` |
| Dense 27B declared W4A4 MLP speed on GB10 | **no** | rests on FP4 MMA; Hopper needs an E2M1 to E4M3 path on the FP8 MMA (exact conversion; group-16 scales against a K=32 FP8 step is the open design question) |
| The lean NVFP4 tensor-core expert decode (#108) | as code, **yes**; as a win, **unmeasured** | it is on main (the C5 squash, 5eafc5c8c) and therefore on this branch: `crates/model-layers/src/layers/moe/nvfp4_lean.rs`, `forward_nvfp4_grouped_decode.rs`, kernels `moe_nvfp4_grouped_tc.cu` / `moe_grouped_tc.cuh` / `tc_weight_formats.cuh`. It is W4A16 on `mma.sync.m16n8k16` BF16 tiles, not an FP4 MMA, so it runs on `sm_90a`. Its measured win (-17..-26 % expert energy, byte-identical) is GB10's unified-memory power regime only: **measure it on Hopper** (the NVFP4 35B's expert decode) rather than assume it; the Hopper end state for experts is a `wgmma` FP8 or W4A8 path |
| The NVFP4 experts on the grouped tensor-core prefill path (#121, `--moe-prefill-tc`) | as code, probably; as a win, **no** | the same `mma.sync` BF16 tiles, so it should assemble for `sm_90a`; it is an open PR (not on this branch), and its wins (prefill, the 17 GiB freed) were measured on GB10 only |
| GB10 serving defaults (`[defaults]`, W8A8 prefill caps, row ladders) | **no** | tuned for 48 SMs and 273 GB/s; Hopper's own rows are copied baselines unless an attribution note measured them |
| GB10 `[benchmarks.limits]`, `[memory] util_ceiling` | **no** | measured facts of the GB10 chassis and its unified memory |
| Hopper attribution wins (`docs/perf/hopper/`) | yes, measured | on 1x H100 for Qwen3.8-27B-FP8; re-confirm on the campaign's box before using as a baseline |

## 4. Models, in order

The campaign runs one model at a time: **hardware, then the Qwen3.8 27B dense model, then the
improvement loop until it wins, then a certification campaign**; then the same process for the
next model, until every model below is certified.

1. **`unsloth/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4` (dense) first**: the best-scoped, least work. On Hopper it runs
   the W4A16 fallback (no FP4 tensor cores), so the first loop is about the W4A16 GEMV/GEMM
   tiers, the GDN recurrent state and paged attention, not about new formats.
2. **`Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8` (MoE)** next: FP8 is native on Hopper, the cleanest MoE
   comparison with vLLM.
3. **`nvidia/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4` (MoE)**: NVFP4 experts on a device with no FP4 MMA.

| Model | Declared formats | Why this position | Golden plans |
|---|---|---|---|
| `unsloth/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4` (dense) | NVFP4 W4A4 MLP (layers 0-55), FP8 MLP (56-63), FP8 attention/GDN, FP8 head | the flagship dense model; exercises the A4 gap and the GDN state that dominates at C >= 64 | `qwen3.8-27b-nvfp4{,-declared}--h100-sxm.md` |
| `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8` (MoE) | FP8 block-128 W8A8 throughout | FP8 is native on Hopper: the cleanest MoE comparison with vLLM | `qwen3.6-35b-a3b-fp8{,-declared}--h100-sxm.md` |
| `nvidia/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4` (MoE) | NVFP4 W4A16 experts and head, FP8 per-tensor attention/GDN | the NVFP4 MoE path on a device with no FP4 MMA | `qwen3.6-35b-a3b-nvfp4-declared--h100-sxm.md` |

Expect vLLM to have trouble with NVFP4 on Hopper (a W4A16 fallback with shape constraints on the
linear-attention projections): if it cannot load or serve a checkpoint, that is a recorded
result, and the FP8 checkpoint is the like-for-like comparison.

## 5. Parameterization opportunities visible from here

- **SKU facts out of HARDWARE.toml**: `sm_count` and the unread `memory_*` keys duplicate
  DEVICES.toml; derive the build constant from DEVICES.toml or probe it at boot.
- **Thresholds as functions of SMs**: the removed `gdn_ba_gates_gemm_batched_twin` rule is a
  96-row threshold that means "two CTAs per SM on 48 SMs"; make the rule's row bound a function
  of `sm_count` and the class-specific `remove` disappears.
- **The `[shadow]` W8A16 GEMVs** (`w8a16_gemv.cu`, `w8a16_gemv_fused.cu` via
  `w8a16_gemv_hopper.cuh`): find what they change against gb10's (tile, vector width,
  pipeline) and make it a tuning policy of one source.
- **Per-head_dim and per-KV-format attention copies** (`paged_decode_attn_*_128.cu`,
  `*_512.cu`) and the class's split-K pair: one template over head_dim and a KV load policy.
- **The FP8 MMA atom as a policy** of the WxAy engine (`mma.sync.m16n8k32.e4m3` today,
  `wgmma` next) rather than new Hopper GEMM files.
- **Roofline floors**: `KERNEL-PERF.md` states floors in GB10 constants; take the peaks from
  DEVICES.toml so the first H100 microbench rows have a meaningful "% of floor".

## 6. What this PR already did (code-free preparation)

- **`met circuit show --hardware <device>` and `met circuit venn --hardware <device>`**, through
  one device argument shared with `plan` and `display`. `show` prints exactly the plan
  `met circuit plan --format plan` prints; `venn` plans every compared model on the device's
  class, costs with the device's roofline and counts only the class's own evidence (on hopper
  today: no measured-shared row at all).
- **A Hopper `[tensor_core_policy]`** (`kernels/hopper/HARDWARE.toml`): gb10's op set required
  on tensor cores from one row; every covered op the Hopper plans run off tensor cores (all
  matrix models, h100-sxm / h100-nvl / h200-sxm, decode, multi_seq 2-128, verify 2-5, draft
  1-32), and every rule of the class's rule set that would, listed as a `backlog` (or `shape`)
  exemption with a Hopper reason. A regression of a tensor-core group to CUDA cores now fails
  `met circuit plan` and the matrix `--check` (checked: dropping one exemption fails the check);
  a removed rule shows up as a stale golden plan. The h100-sxm and h200-sxm golden plans carry
  the policy section. It cannot yet cover the class's own tensor-core kernels (audit item 6):
  no plan runs them. The backlog entries are the work list: W4A16 at 1-3 rows, the BF16 head
  and router GEMVs, paged decode attention, the GDN beta/decay projection, the FP8 MoE experts
  (native FP8 MMA on Hopper makes these the FP8 35B's first gap), the batched draft head, and the
  BF16 head above 8 rows.
- **`[benchmarks.limits]`: deliberately NOT declared.** Measure on the H100.
- **The mock path is current with main** (the base, `feat/ml-utils`).
